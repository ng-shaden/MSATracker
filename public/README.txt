MSATRACKER – PERSONAL DATA EXPORT

This archive contains a copy of the personal data associated with your MSATracker account, as held by MSATracker at the time the export was generated.

1. PERSONAL DATA INCLUDED

The archive may contain the following categories of personal data:

- Account information: Your account identifier, email address, username, name and profile picture.
- Personal preferences: Your account-specific display and interface preferences.
- Favorites: Media titles you have added to your favorites.
- Media tracking information: Your watchlist, currently watching and completed lists, including relevant dates and status information.

2. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

Your personal data is processed to provide and maintain MSATracker's services, including account management, authentication, personalization and media tracking functionality.

3. DATA SOURCES

Account and profile information is primarily provided by you.

Personal preferences, favorites and media tracking information are generated through your use of MSATracker.

Media catalogue identifiers and related catalogue information are obtained from The Movie Database (TMDB).

4. DATA RECIPIENTS

Your personal data may be processed by third-party service providers that support MSATracker's operation, such as hosting, database and authentication providers.

Further information about these providers and any applicable international data transfers is available in the MSATracker Privacy Policy.

5. DATA RETENTION

Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to provide MSATracker's services and fulfil applicable legal obligations.

Further information about retention periods and the criteria used to determine them is available in the MSATracker Privacy Policy.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data.
- Request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Request the erasure of your personal data.
- Request restriction of processing.
- Object to certain types of processing.
- Receive or request the transfer of your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, where the right to data portability applies.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

For further information or to exercise your rights, please consult the MSATracker Privacy Policy.

7. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

This export is provided in JSON format to allow the information to be read and processed by commonly available software.

The export reflects the personal data included in MSATracker's export process at the time it was generated. It does not necessarily include every record of processing or technical log maintained by third-party service providers.
